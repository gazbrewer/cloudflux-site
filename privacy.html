2. Our two roles

  • We are the controller for personal data about our own prospects and customers, for example the tradespeople who contact us through this website, sign up for a trial or buy the service.
  • We are a processor for personal data about the people who call or message our clients' businesses, such as a householder ringing a plumber. The tradesperson (our client) is the controller of that data. We process it only on their instructions, under a written Data Processing Agreement (UK GDPR Article 28). If you called a business that uses our service, please contact that business first about your data. We'll help them respond.

4. The receptionist service (we are processor)

For our clients, the service may process: the caller's name, phone number, address and postcode, details of the job or problem, call audio recordings, transcripts and summaries, message content, and booking details. Callers are told at the start that they're speaking to an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. Our clients decide whether recording is on, and they're responsible for having a lawful basis (usually legitimate interests, or steps at the caller's request before a contract). We don't use callers' data for our own marketing, and we don't sell it.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to processing (including direct marketing, at any time), data portability, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To use these rights, email privacy@cloudflux.uk. We'll reply within one month. If we process your data for one of our clients, we'll pass your request to them and help them respond.

8. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, two-factor authentication on our accounts, least-privilege access and supplier due diligence. If there's a personal data breach, we'll handle it as the law requires, and we'll tell affected clients without undue delay.

9. Automated decisions

The receptionist sorts enquiries by urgency and offers appointment slots. It does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Our clients make the final decisions on jobs and prices.

10. Complaints

Please contact us first and we'll try to fix it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.
