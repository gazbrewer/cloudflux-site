Data processing terms (DPA)

UK GDPR Article 28 · Last updated: 12 November 2026

These terms apply when a business ("the Client") signs up to the Never miss a job AI receptionist service provided by Cloudflux Limited, a private limited company registered in England and Wales, company number 16552311, registered office Mansion House, Manchester Road, Altrincham, Cheshire, WA14 4RW ("Cloudflux"). By ticking the box on the sign-up form, the Client agrees to these terms. They form part of the agreement for the service ("the Main Agreement").

1. Roles, subject matter and duration

For personal data about the Client's callers and customers, the Client is the controller and Cloudflux is the processor. Cloudflux provides an AI receptionist that answers calls for the Client, captures enquiry details and sends call summaries to the Client. These terms last as long as the Main Agreement, and after that for as long as Cloudflux holds Client personal data.

2. Processing details

3. Cloudflux's obligations

Cloudflux will:

  1. Process personal data only on the Client's documented instructions (these terms, the Main Agreement and the Client's configuration choices), including for international transfers, unless the law requires otherwise. If so, Cloudflux will tell the Client first unless the law forbids it.
  2. Tell the Client straight away if it believes an instruction breaks data protection law.
  3. Make sure everyone authorised to process the data is bound by confidentiality.
  4. Put in place appropriate technical and organisational measures (Article 32), including encryption in transit, access controls with multi-factor authentication, least-privilege access, supplier due diligence and logging that avoids caller details.
  5. Only use sub-processors under section 4.
  6. Help the Client respond to data subject rights requests, taking into account the nature of the processing.
  7. Help the Client meet its Articles 32–36 obligations (security, breach notification, DPIAs, prior consultation).
  8. Notify the Client of a personal data breach without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours of becoming aware of it, with the information the Client reasonably needs.
  9. When the service ends, delete or return all personal data, at the Client's choice, within 30 days, unless the law requires it to be kept.
  10. Make available the information needed to show compliance with Article 28, and allow and contribute to reasonable audits on 30 days' notice, no more than once a year unless there has been a breach.

5. International transfers

Personal data is only transferred outside the UK under UK adequacy regulations or appropriate safeguards (the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses), with a transfer risk assessment where needed.

6. The Client's responsibilities

The Client confirms it has a lawful basis for the processing and will make sure callers are informed, including by mentioning the AI receptionist and call recording in its own privacy notice. The receptionist tells every caller at the start of the call that it is an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. The Client will not instruct Cloudflux to process data unlawfully.

7. Liability, precedence and law

Liability is as set out in the Main Agreement. If these terms and the Main Agreement conflict on data protection, these terms apply. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
