What it handles

Set up for how trades actually work.

  • Emergency triage. Sorts burst pipes, no heating and no power from routine quotes, and flags them urgent to you.
  • Safety first. Tells a caller with a leak how to turn off the stopcock.
  • Books visits. Offers slots from your calendar and never double-books.
  • Never quotes prices unless you tell it to. It takes the details so you can quote properly.
  • Calls, texts and web chat, answered 24/7, with replies in under a minute.
  • Sounds local. A natural British voice, set up with your business name and your service area.
⚠️ Gas safety handoff

If a caller says they can smell gas, the receptionist doesn't book anything. It tells them to get out of the property, not to touch switches or flames, and to ring the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. Then it alerts you.

FAQ

Questions trades ask us

Will customers know it's AI?

Yes. It tells callers at the start that it's an AI assistant answering for your business. People mostly just want the problem sorted, and being upfront builds trust.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your number and set your phone to divert unanswered or busy calls. We'll help you set it up.

Are calls recorded? What about consent?

Calls can be recorded and transcribed so you get an accurate summary. The receptionist tells callers at the start that the call may be recorded. You can turn recording off if you'd rather not.

Is it GDPR compliant?

We built it with UK GDPR in mind. For your callers' data, you're the controller and Cloudflux acts as your processor. We provide a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), store data in the UK or EU where possible, keep only what's needed, and delete it on a set schedule. See our privacy notice.

Where is the data stored?

In the UK or EU where possible. Some suppliers, like the voice and telephony providers, may process data elsewhere under UK-approved safeguards such as the International Data Transfer Agreement. These are listed in the DPA.

What if it gets something wrong?

You get a summary of every conversation, so nothing slips through. During the trial we review calls with you every week and adjust what it says.

What happens with a gas leak or a real emergency?

For a gas smell it never books a visit. It gives the standard safety advice and directs the caller to the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. For other urgent jobs it gives simple safety steps and flags the lead to you straight away.

Can I cancel?

Yes, with 30 days' notice, and there's no long contract. The free trial has no commitment at all.
